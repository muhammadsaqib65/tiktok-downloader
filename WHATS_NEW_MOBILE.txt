🎯 HOW TO USE THE NEW FEATURES
================================================================================

INSTALL ON MOBILE:

iPhone (Safari):
  1. Open Safari → go to your app URL
  2. Tap Share → "Add to Home Screen"
  3. Tap "Add"
  4. Find icon on home screen
  5. Tap to open as app!

Android (Chrome):
  1. Open Chrome → go to your app URL
  2. Tap "Install" button at bottom
  3. Tap "Install" to confirm
  4. Find in app drawer
  5. Tap to open as app!

SHARE FROM TIKTOK:

  1. Open TikTok app
  2. Find any video
  3. Tap Share button (↗️)
  4. Select "TikDL" or your app name
  5. App opens with URL auto-filled!
  6. Downloads automatically!
  7. Done in 10 seconds! 🎉

BEFORE vs AFTER:

Before (6 steps, 1 minute):
  TikTok → Copy → Exit → Browser → Navigate → Paste → Download

After (3 steps, 10 seconds):
  TikTok → Share → Done! ✨

Savings: 3 steps, 50 seconds per video!

🔧 THE FIX EXPLAINED
================================================================================

PROBLEM:
  - Sharing from TikTok showed "0.0.0.0 refused to connect"
  - App tried to redirect using server-side URL (0.0.0.0)
  - Didn't work on real devices

SOLUTION:
  ✅ Changed to GET method in manifest
  ✅ Created client-side share page
  ✅ Uses browser redirects (not server)
  ✅ Works with any domain/deployment
  ✅ No hardcoded URLs

FILES CHANGED:
  - public/manifest.json (POST → GET)
  - src/app/share/route.ts (deleted - not needed)
  - src/app/share/page.tsx (new - client-side)

RESULT:
  ✅ Share works on Android
  ✅ Share works on iOS (limited)
  ✅ No more connection errors
  ✅ Proper URL handling

🚀 DEPLOYMENT REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

CRITICAL:
  ⚠️ MUST use HTTPS (not HTTP)
  ⚠️ MUST be publicly accessible
  ⚠️ localhost won't work for sharing

RECOMMENDED PLATFORMS:
  1. Vercel (best, free HTTPS)
  2. Railway (auto database)
  3. Render (free tier)
  4. Netlify (easy setup)

ENVIRONMENT VARIABLES:
  Required:
    - DATABASE_URL (PostgreSQL)
  
  Optional:
    - RAPIDAPI_KEY (better reliability)
