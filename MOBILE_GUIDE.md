## 📲 Installation Guide

### For iPhone (iOS/Safari)

#### Step 1: Open the App in Safari
1. Open **Safari** browser (must use Safari, not Chrome)
2. Go to your app URL: `https://your-app-url.com`

#### Step 2: Add to Home Screen
1. Tap the **Share** button (square with arrow pointing up) at the bottom
2. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
3. Edit the name if you want (default: "TikDL")
4. Tap **"Add"** in the top right

#### Step 3: Open the App
1. Go to your home screen
2. Find the "TikDL" icon
3. Tap to open - it opens like a native app!

#### Step 4: Enable Sharing from TikTok
1. Open **TikTok** app
2. Find a video you want to download
3. Tap **Share** button
4. Scroll right and tap **"More"**
5. Find **"TikDL"** or your app name
6. Tap to share - it opens in the downloader!

**Note:** On iOS, you may need to share once from Safari first before it appears in TikTok's share menu.

---

### For Android (Chrome/Edge)

#### Step 1: Open the App in Chrome
1. Open **Chrome** or **Edge** browser
2. Go to your app URL: `https://your-app-url.com`

#### Step 2: Install the App
**Method 1: Automatic Prompt**
1. You'll see a pop-up at the bottom: **"Install App"**
2. Tap **"Install"**
3. The app is now installed!

**Method 2: Manual Install**
1. Tap the **3 dots menu** (⋮) in the top right
2. Tap **"Install app"** or **"Add to Home screen"**
3. Tap **"Install"** in the confirmation dialog

#### Step 3: Open the App
1. Find the app icon on your home screen or app drawer
2. Tap to open - works like a native app!

#### Step 4: Share from TikTok
1. Open **TikTok** app
2. Find a video you want to download
3. Tap the **Share** button (arrow icon)
4. Look for **"TikDL"** or your app name in the share sheet
5. Tap it - the video URL is automatically shared to the downloader!
6. Video downloads without watermark!

**First Time Setup:**
- The first time you share, Android will ask which app to use
- Select "TikDL" and optionally choose "Always" for future shares

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### "Install" button doesn't appear (Android)

**Solutions:**
1. Make sure you're using Chrome or Edge
2. Clear browser cache and reload
3. Check you're on HTTPS (not HTTP)
4. Use the 3-dot menu → "Add to Home screen"

### App doesn't appear in TikTok share menu (iOS)

**Solutions:**
1. Share from Safari to the app once first
2. Restart TikTok app
3. Check iOS version (needs iOS 13+)
4. Try sharing to Safari first, then to the app

### App doesn't appear in TikTok share menu (Android)

**Solutions:**
1. Install the app first (not just "Add to Home screen")
2. Clear TikTok app cache
3. Restart TikTok app
4. Update Android WebView component

### Video won't download

**Solutions:**
1. Check internet connection
2. Make sure URL is valid TikTok link
3. Try a different video (private videos don't work)
4. Clear app cache and retry

### App opens in browser instead of standalone

**Solutions:**
1. Delete home screen icon
2. Reinstall using the proper method
3. Make sure you tapped "Install" not just "Add to Home screen"
