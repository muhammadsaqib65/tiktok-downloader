# 📱 Mobile Quick Start - Install in 2 Minutes!

**Get the TikTok Downloader on your phone NOW!**

---

## 🚀 Super Fast Install

### iPhone (iOS) - Safari Only

1. **Open Safari** (must use Safari, not Chrome)
2. **Go to your app URL**
3. **Tap Share button** (square with ↑ arrow)
4. **Tap "Add to Home Screen"**
5. **Tap "Add"**
6. **Done!** Find icon on home screen

**Time: 30 seconds**

---

### Android - Chrome or Edge

1. **Open Chrome** or Edge browser
2. **Go to your app URL**
3. **Tap "Install"** button at bottom
   - OR tap ⋮ menu → "Install app"
4. **Tap "Install"** to confirm
5. **Done!** Find icon in app drawer

**Time: 20 seconds**

---

## 🎬 Share from TikTok (The Magic Part!)

### After Installing:

1. **Open TikTok app**
2. **Find any video**
3. **Tap Share button** ↗️
4. **Select "TikDL"** (or your app name)
5. **Video downloads automatically!** ✨

**No copy-paste needed!**

---

## 💡 First Time Sharing

**Android:**
- First share: Android asks which app to use
- Choose "TikDL"
- Select "Always" for one-tap future downloads

**iPhone:**
- Share may go to Safari first
- Then tap "Open in TikDL" if prompted
- Or manually copy link and paste in app

## 🐛 Not Working?

### Can't install?
- Use Chrome (Android) or Safari (iOS)
- Check you're on HTTPS URL
- Try clearing browser cache

### Share button missing?
- Reinstall the app
- Restart TikTok app
- Try sharing to browser first

### Videos won't download?
- Check internet connection
- Try different video (public only)
- Reload the app
