🔬 ADVANCED: LIGHTHOUSE TEST
================================================================================

This will tell you EXACTLY what's wrong:

1. Open: https://tiktok-downloader-jet-delta.vercel.app/
2. Press F12

Look at the score:

Common errors:
  "Manifest not found" → Upload public/manifest.json
  "Service worker not registered" → Upload public/sw.js
  "Icons not found" → Upload public/icon-192.png and icon-512.png
